Data Privacy and Authorization Policy

Please read carefully before proceeding

  1. It is the policy of the school that whatever information it gathers; will be collected, processed and stored pursuant to the general principles of transparency, legitimate purpose, and proportionality, as mandated by Republic Act No. 10173 or the Data Privacy Act of 2012, its Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR), and other relevant privacy policies issued by the National Privacy Commission.

  2. As a rule, an authorization is valid only for a specific request. If the owner wishes to authorize the same person to request for another set of documents in the future, then another letter of authorization must be issued (Article XX SCHOOL RECORDS AND TRANSFER OF STUDENT Section 97-Release of Records Education Law and the Private Schools, Ulpiano 'ULAN' P. Sarmiento IIII, Revised Expanded Edition - 2009).
